# FRESHNESS POLICY

- **Live-only:** jadwal, slot, availability, status transaksi, status guarantee/approval, current contact routing jika terhubung sistem live.
- **High-change:** BPJS tariff/rules, insurer network/rules, provider schedules — verifikasi sebelum tindakan.
- **Regulatory:** gunakan `verified_as_of` dan official source catalog; jangan menganggap ringkasan lama tetap berlaku.
- **Stable:** terminology, architecture, generic workflow, approved static profile; tetap tunduk pada superseding source.
- **Archive:** tidak boleh mengalahkan sumber aktif.
